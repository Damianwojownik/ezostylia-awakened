# Ezostylia: Awakened
## The World's First AI-Powered Spiritual RPG

**Awakened** is a groundbreaking online adventure that bridges ancient esoteric wisdom (Matrix of Destiny, Tarot, Numerology) with generative AI technology.

### Project Vision
Redefining the $4.5 trillion wellness market by moving beyond static apps into "Synthetic Reality" — where your spiritual growth is an immersive, personalized epic adventure.

### Key Features
- **AI-Driven Storytelling:** A persistent AI Mentor that remembers your journey and adapts the world to your psychological profile.
- **Matrix of Destiny Integration:** Your birth chart becomes your character's source code and gameplay logic.
- **Shadow Work Missions:** Face your inner challenges in a safe, gamified RPG environment.
- **Multiplayer Sangha:** Journey with others to explore group dynamics and authentic connection.
